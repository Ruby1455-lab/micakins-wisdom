Our Story

A decade of building trust, one closing at a time

Micakins Wisdom Real Estate Services was founded on the belief that Nigerian real estate deserves the same rigor and transparency clients expect anywhere else in the world. What began as a small consulting practice in Lagos has grown into a full-service advisory covering estate consulting, agency, facility management, and building material supply — serving families relocating home, first-time buyers, and diaspora investors managing property from afar.

Every listing we bring to market and every client we advise passes through the same disciplined process: verified documentation, on-site inspection, and honest counsel — even when that means advising against a deal.
